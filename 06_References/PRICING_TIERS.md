# Aurora Launch - Pricing Tiers

**Status:** Accepted (S009 closed 2026-05-04)
**Authority:** PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES Section "Pricing Boundaries" (initial) + этот документ (final)
**Sprint context:** Sprint B4 commercial ship + Sprint B6 pilot pricing
**Owner:** Антон (commercial)

## Контекст

Aurora Launch subscription model: unlimited launches per год + consulting hours bundle. Этот документ финализирует:

1. Tier numbers (Starter / Pro / Enterprise)
2. Included features per tier
3. Free trial / pilot offer structure
4. Discount policy (multi-year, Suite bundle, loyalty)
5. Payment structure (annual / quarterly, RUB / USD)
6. Renewal logic (auto-renewal, notice period, grace)
7. Suite bundle pricing relative к individual products

**Note:** prices в этом документе - Phase B initial. Phase C revision возможна после pilot data (Sprint B6+).

## 1. Tier Structure

### 1.1 Starter - 1,500,000 ₽/год

**Target customer:** small-mid agency или brand team запускающий 1-3 launch/year.

**Включено:**
- Unlimited launches per год (Aurora Launch software access)
- 20 часов consulting (proxy review sessions + posterior update sessions + methodology questions + training)
- Email support 48-hour response time
- До 2 user seats (one license = до 2 одновременных пользователей)
- Aurora Launch Methodology Certificate PDF (single-page, Starter format)
- Help system + knowledge base access
- Quarterly product updates

**Не включено:**
- Priority support
- Custom training
- On-site sessions
- White-label deliverables
- API access

**Use case:** "Запускаю 1-2 бренда в год, нужен один эксперт-консультант для первого launch."

### 1.2 Pro - 2,500,000 ₽/год

**Target customer:** mid-large agency или brand team запускающий 4-8 launches/year.

**Включено:** всё из Starter PLUS:
- 30 часов consulting (vs 20h Starter)
- Priority support 24-hour response time
- До 5 user seats
- Quarterly review session (1.5h ≈ 5 of 30 consulting hours - structured review)
- Methodology Certificate PDF (2-page Pro format - includes detailed math + diagnostics)
- Pre-test creative integration support (если customer имеет Kantar Link / Ipsos copytest)
- Optimization scenarios appendix в reports

**Use case:** "Запускаем regularly, нужна быстрая поддержка + детальная методология для CFO defense."

### 1.3 Enterprise - 3,500,000 ₽/год

**Target customer:** large pharma / FMCG / cosmetics brand owner или premium agency.

**Включено:** всё из Pro PLUS:
- 40 часов consulting (vs 30h Pro)
- Dedicated success manager (Антон лично или senior specialist)
- Unlimited user seats
- Custom training (1-2 days on-site session, MSK/SPB - travel при необходимости billed separately)
- Custom analytics dashboards (per customer's KPIs)
- Quarterly + annual review sessions (~10h из 40 - included)
- White-label deliverables (Aurora wordmark replaceable)
- API access (Phase C+, currently waitlist)
- Custom contract terms (NDA, data residency, SLA)

**Use case:** "Стратегический партнёр, мульти-launch portfolio, regulatory environment (pharma)."

## 2. Free Trial / Pilot Offer

**Two paths offered, customer chooses one at sales conversation:**

### 2.1 Path A - 60-day evaluation

- 60 calendar days от signing
- Full Pro tier features (best showcase)
- 5 hours consulting included (instead of 30 - проportional)
- Up to 1 launch project execution
- Unlimited при no launch executed (just exploration)
- Conversion offer: choose Starter/Pro/Enterprise tier при 60-day end
- Auto-cancel если no decision by day 60

**For:** customers wanting low-commitment evaluation.

### 2.2 Path B - Pilot first launch FREE

- One complete launch project executed гладко с Aurora team
- Full Methodology Certificate delivered
- Customer commits к case study consent (anonymized, Aurora marketing rights)
- Pilot duration: ~12 weeks (one launch cycle)
- Conversion: at end, customer decides tier к sign full subscription

**For:** customers с specific brand launch in pipeline + open к marketing partnership.

**Both paths:** no charge до conversion. Aurora team (Антон) consulting hours fully provided pilot period.

## 3. Discount Policy

### 3.1 Multi-year Commitment

- 1 year: standard price (no discount)
- 2 years: -5% on total contract value
- 3 years: -10% on total contract value
- 4+ years: -15% (Enterprise only, custom negotiation)

Payment options для multi-year:
- Annual upfront with discount applied to year 1 (later years priced at locked rate)
- Total upfront with full discount applied (rare)

### 3.2 Suite Bundle Discount

При покупке нескольких Aurora products (per Suite strategy):

- Aurora Launch + Aurora Optimize: -25% off combined
- Aurora Launch + Aurora Optimize + Aurora Brand: -40% off combined (Suite Bundle)
- Add-ons (Pricing/Promo Phase C+): -10% incremental к existing bundle

**Example:** Pro tier Aurora Launch (2.5M) + Pro tier Aurora Optimize (estimated 1.5M) + Pro tier Aurora Brand (estimated 2.0M) = 6.0M individually → 3.6M Suite Bundle (40% off).

### 3.3 Loyalty Renewal Discount

Per tier (per Section 1):
- Starter: -10% при renewal
- Pro: -15% при renewal
- Enterprise: -20% при renewal

Compounds год за year (capped at 30% maximum for Enterprise multi-year clients).

### 3.4 Pilot Conversion Discount (one-time)

Customer prefer Pilot Path B и converts within 30 days of pilot completion:
- -10% on first year subscription (any tier)
- Can stack с multi-year commitment

### 3.5 Volume Discount (multiple parallel projects)

Single customer, multiple parallel launch projects (rare, mostly Enterprise):
- 5+ parallel projects: -10% incremental on additional Enterprise seats
- Custom-quoted

## 4. Payment Structure

### 4.1 Currencies

- **Primary:** RUB. All prices в этом документе - в рублях.
- **Secondary:** USD доступен для Enterprise contracts при international entity или customer preference. Conversion at signing date Russian Central Bank rate, fixed для contract duration.

### 4.2 Payment Schedule

**Annual upfront (default):**
- 100% payment within 30 days of signing
- Most cost-effective option (no surcharge)

**Quarterly billing (Pro+ only):**
- 25% billed quarterly
- 5% surcharge на total annual cost (compensates float + admin)
- Available для Pro и Enterprise tiers (not Starter)

**Custom (Enterprise only):**
- Negotiated milestones
- Linked к customer fiscal calendar
- No surcharge if total annual paid within calendar year

### 4.3 Payment Terms

- Net 30 days от invoice date
- Late payment: 1.5% per month interest (по российскому законодательству)
- Sustained late payment (>60 days): service suspension + reconciliation fee 50K ₽

## 5. Renewal Logic

### 5.1 Auto-Renewal Default

- Auto-renewal ON по default
- Customer notified 90 days перед renewal date
- Renewal at same tier по default

### 5.2 Non-Renewal Notice

- 60 days written notice required для opt-out
- Notice via email к ackold@yandex.ru OR contractual signing party
- After notice: service continues till contract end, then access ceases

### 5.3 Tier Changes

**Upgrade:**
- Available anytime
- Pro-rated charge for remainder of billing year
- New tier benefits effective immediately
- Loyalty discount (Section 3.3) carries over но caps at upgraded tier rate

**Downgrade:**
- Effective from next billing cycle (no mid-year downgrade)
- Notice period: 30 days перед next billing
- Loyalty discount may reset (новый tier baseline)

### 5.4 Grace Period

- 30 days grace period для late payment перед service suspension
- During grace: full feature access, но Aurora team prompts payment
- After grace + 30 days non-payment: account suspension (read-only access к existing projects)
- After 90 days total non-payment: account closure + data export option (90 days)

### 5.5 Data Retention

- Active subscription: full data retention в Aurora Launch local + cloud sync (если opt-in)
- Suspended account: read-only retention (90 days)
- Closed account: data export prompt (90 days), then deletion per DATA_PRIVACY policy

## 6. Pricing Sensitivity Analysis (rationale)

### 6.1 Why 1.5M Starter (not lower)

- Below 1.5M, sales motion economics don't work (Антон lone founder cannot sustain low-margin clients)
- 1.5M = ~$15K USD при current rates, on par с Nielsen BASES per-launch pricing of $5-15M (Aurora 5-10× cheaper)
- 20h consulting included = ~75K ₽/h equivalent (premium rate, sustainable)

### 6.2 Why 2.5M Pro (not 2M или 3M)

- 2M: not enough margin для priority support overhead
- 3M: too close к Enterprise gap closes
- 2.5M positions middle of Nielsen BASES alternative ($25K USD) but premium vs in-house эконометрист (~1.5M one-time per project)

### 6.3 Why 3.5M Enterprise (not 5M+)

- 3.5M = ~$35K USD - competitive with high-end Kantar/Ipsos consulting projects (1-3M one-time per project, multi-launch context)
- Above 5M, customers would expect on-site team or full custom build
- Includes dedicated success manager - real cost for Aurora ~10-15h/month attention by Антон or senior

### 6.4 Phase C Revision Possibilities

Pricing may revise (новый ADR при изменении):
- Lower при scale (1000+ customers, sales automation): possible 20-30% reduction
- Higher если white-label demand strong: Enterprise +50% premium tier
- Inflation-linked: annual indexing к ЦБ inflation rate (typical 6-10% in РФ)
